# KeenSight v4.1 — all 52 ledger ideas and their end-to-end placement

## Basis and interpretation

This mapping preserves the supplied ledger's IDs and categories. “Already Have,” cost/effort ratings, source-access claims, performance claims and causal interpretations remain **unverified brainstorming metadata**. They are not imported into normative business facts. This task used the supplied materials; it did not verify provider pricing, accounts or licenses.

**Sources yield evidence; extraction yields attributable facts; computations yield derived measurements or hypotheses; ranking, filters, playbooks and copy strategies are software/configuration, not business observations.** Case-study results concern the comparison organization. Product/industry priors remain internal context and cannot prove the target company's pain.

The patch retains all 197 v4 predicates and adds 16 closed record families. The full catalog now has 213 predicate definitions and 89 metrics. The new source collectors, eight compound algorithms, embedding index, scorecards, case-study copy renderers and playbook catalog are not implemented or automatically enabled. The supplied workflow still has three constrained preview renderers.

The `LookalikeMatch` and `ScenarioEstimate` schemas are explicitly DESIGN_ONLY output specifications. No production or arbitrary cross-organization-copy path accepts them. The Automation Playbooks tab referenced by row 52 was not supplied.

## Explicitly rejected promotions

- Posting removal is not evidence that hiring failed or a project was cancelled.
- Missing public vendor traces do not disprove an AI claim or prove missing private automation.
- A complaint sample does not measure true missed-call volume, lost revenue or an account's actual operational failure rate.
- A financing filing, historic loan or sponsorship does not prove a current spending budget or propensity to buy.
- A numeric case-study result is not automatically credible, causal, transferable, or our own customer result.
- Public source visibility does not grant access, storage, LLM-processing or outreach rights.
- Algorithms, template strategies and playbook mappings do not become OBSERVED facts simply because their outputs are persisted.

## Activated boundaries

The schema additions allow the new evidence families to be registered and tested. Actual capture, extraction, compound evaluation and copy activation each require their own enabled profile and validated implementation. All supplied providers/actor grants/destinations are synthetic. Sending remains disabled.
